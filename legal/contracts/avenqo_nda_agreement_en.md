### SECTION 1 — DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
"**Confidential Information**" encompasses all documents, records, business data, trade secrets, source code, tool architectures, accounting records, uploaded datasets, conversation transcripts, or technical specifications disclosed by one Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") to the other (the "**Receiving Party**"), whether transmitted in writing, orally, electronically, via API interfaces, or through direct access to the Avenqo platform.

### SECTION 2 — NON-DISCLOSURE AND SECURITY COVENANTS
The Receiving Party covenants and agrees to:
1. Maintain strict confidentiality of all Confidential Information, applying at least a reasonable degree of care and a level of security no less protective than that exercised for its own sensitive information of like importance.
2. Refrain from disclosing, directly or indirectly, any Confidential Information to third parties without prior express written authorization from the Disclosing Party.
3. Use Confidential Information solely for the legitimate purposes of technology evaluation, onboarding, personalization, and execution of Avenqo platform services.
4. Restrict access strictly to employees, executives, consultants, and approved contractors with a bona fide need-to-know, who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as rigorous as those set forth herein.

### SECTION 3 — EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIALITY
Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:
- Was already part of the public domain at the time of disclosure or subsequently became public without breach of this Agreement.
- Was already lawfully in its possession prior to disclosure, free of any duty of confidentiality.
- Was lawfully obtained from an independent third party possessing unencumbered rights of disclosure.
- Was independently developed by its personnel without reference to or reliance upon the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.
- Is required to be disclosed pursuant to a lawful judicial subpoena or statutory obligation (provided the Receiving Party promptly notifies the Disclosing Party to allow an opportunity to seek an appropriate protective order, to the extent permitted by law).

### SECTION 4 — DATA PROTECTION AND RESTRICTION ON PUBLIC AI TRAINING (LAW 25 & PIPEDA)
4.1 **Client Exclusive Ownership**: The Client remains the sole proprietor of all legal, proprietary, and beneficial rights in and to its enterprise records, catalogs, transactions, and business files.
4.2 **No Public AI Model Training**: PMC Solutions AI strictly covenants that Client business data and Confidential Information shall **NEVER** be utilized to train, tune, or augment generalized, public, or shared foundation AI models without prior express written consent.
4.3 **Security Safeguards**: PMC Solutions AI deploys industry-standard organizational and technical safeguards, including transport layer encryption (TLS 1.3) and storage encryption (AES-256). No absolute guarantee of zero technical risk or uninterrupted security is stipulated or implied.

### SECTION 5 — TERM AND SURVIVAL
This Agreement takes effect upon acceptance for an initial term of two (2) years. Confidentiality obligations with respect to manufacturing and commercial trade secrets shall survive indefinitely, for as long as the underlying information retains its legal status as a trade secret under applicable law.
