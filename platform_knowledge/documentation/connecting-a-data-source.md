title: Connecting a business data source
tags: [connections, data, integration]
---

Avenqo needs at least one connected data source (like a CSV export or a supported integration) before it can answer questions about your business or run predictions.

To connect a data source, go to **Connections** and follow the guided setup. Once connected, Avenqo's Business Assistant can analyze it, and predictive tools (forecasting, churn risk, anomalies) become available once enough historical data is present.
