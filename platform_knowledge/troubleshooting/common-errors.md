id: troubleshooting-common-errors
title: Common error messages and what they mean
tags: [errors, troubleshooting]
---

- **"We couldn't import this file"**: usually means a required column (like date or amount) is missing or the file format isn't recognized. Check the CSV template and try again.
- **"This prediction isn't available yet"**: the underlying model needs more historical data, or hasn't finished training yet.
- **"Avenqo AI is temporarily unavailable"**: a temporary issue with the AI service. This is not a data problem — simply try again in a moment.
- **"You've reached your plan's usage limit"**: your company has reached an AI usage quota for this billing period. An account owner can review usage under Billing.
