title: What's included in each plan?
tags: [plans, pricing, features, faq]
---

Avenqo has three plan tiers: **Demo**, **Professional**, and **Enterprise** (Enterprise customers may also be on a **Custom Enterprise** agreement). Each plan unlocks a different set of modules and AI capabilities.

To see exactly what your company's current plan includes, ask "What's included in my plan?" — Avenqo Support only shares your own company's plan and enabled capabilities, never pricing details for other customers.
