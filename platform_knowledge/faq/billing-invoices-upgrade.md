tags: [billing, invoices, upgrade, plan, faq]
---

Your billing information lives on the **Billing** page inside Avenqo (accessible from the main navigation). From there you can:

- See your current plan and subscription status.
- View and download your invoices (number, date, amount, status, PDF link).
- Open the secure Stripe billing portal to update your payment method or cancel.
- Upgrade from **Demo** to **Professional** directly with Stripe Checkout. **Enterprise** requires contacting the Avenqo sales team for a custom agreement.

If your AI access has stopped, it's most often because your company's monthly AI usage limit for your current plan has been reached — ask "What plan am I on?" or "Has my AI usage limit been reached?" to check. Upgrading your plan increases these limits.

Avenqo Support never modifies your billing directly — all billing changes happen through the Billing page or the Stripe portal.
