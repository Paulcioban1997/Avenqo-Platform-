title: What's new — Avenqo Support AI and reliability improvements
tags: [release-notes, support, reliability]
---

Avenqo now has a dedicated **Support assistant** to answer questions about how to use the product (imports, connections, plans, and common error messages) — separate from your Business Assistant, and it never accesses your company's business data.

Avenqo's AI has also become more reliable: if one AI provider is temporarily slow or unavailable, Avenqo can automatically continue the conversation using another configured provider, without you noticing which one is being used.
