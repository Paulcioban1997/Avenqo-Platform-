title: How to use the Avenqo Business Assistant
tags: [assistant, chat, guide]
---

The **Assistant** page lets you ask questions about your own business in plain language, like "How are my sales performing?" or "Which customers need attention?". Avenqo looks at your connected data and answers directly, with sources you can expand.

This is different from **Avenqo Support**, which answers questions about how to use the Avenqo product itself (for example, how to import a file or what a plan includes) and never has access to your business data.
