# ADR-001: Modular SaaS Architecture for PMC Solutions AI Platform

## Status
Accepted

## Context
The platform must support multiple AI product lines and future growth without becoming a tightly coupled monolith.

## Decision
Adopt a modular architecture centered on clear boundaries between frontend, gateway, backend, modules, agents, shared services, and data concerns.

## Why this architecture?
It keeps the system extensible, maintainable, and easier to evolve as new product domains are introduced.

## Why modular?
Modules allow separate product tasks to grow independently while preserving a coherent platform foundation.

## Why independent modules?
Independence reduces cross-team coupling and makes integration safer.

## Why AI Agents?
Agents provide a dedicated abstraction layer for role-based intelligence and specialized automation.

## Why an API Gateway?
A gateway centralizes routing, security boundaries, and cross-cutting concerns before they reach the core services.
